

## **Enhancing Student Engagement and Campus Operations at Durham College with AIDAP**

Durham College is advancing its digital transformation by adopting the AI-Powered Digital Assistant Platform (AIDAP) to serve as a centralized tool for students, faculty, and administrative staff to access academic information and campus services easily. With a large population of over 10,000 students and numerous staff members, Durham College faces challenges such as students missing important deadlines due to fragmented communication, staff burdened with manual administrative work, and leadership lacking real-time campus-wide insights.

AIDAP will provide students at Durham College with quick, personalized responses to their academic questions, send timely alerts about schedule changes, and deliver custom notifications accessible via mobile, web, or voice channels. This will help students stay organized, reduce stress, and improve academic performance. Faculty and staff will gain from automation of announcements, automated reminders for assignments and exams, and simplified access to grading analytics, enabling them to focus more on teaching and student support.

Administrators will benefit from AIDAP’s integration of multiple institutional data sources such as registration systems, learning management systems, and email platforms, enabling streamlined data management and policy enforcement with less manual effort. The platform’s strong compliance with privacy and security standards protects sensitive information while supporting institutional goals.

By implementing AIDAP, Durham College expects to improve operational efficiency, foster digital skills in its community, and build a flexible system that can grow with the institution. The platform will save time, minimize communication errors, enhance service quality, and help Durham College maintain its position as a modern, student-centered educational institution ready to meet future challenges.
